## Post

Your agent's 'health score' is probably lying to you.

A 99.7% success rate looks identical whether the endpoint is rate-limiting you or the network path is silently broken. The dashboard says green. The reality is silent back-pressure. The fix for one makes the other worse.

Most agent observability stacks I see are success rate plus p99 latency. Two gauges and no warning lights. That is a control panel for a system that fails slowly and quietly.

The unlock is to pair success rate with fast-changing transport signals: TCP reset bursts, ICMP reachability, retry storms. They are cheap to collect, they move on the order of seconds, and they differentiate between 'endpoint unhappy' and 'path dead' -- which success rate alone never will.

Open research question: what is the minimum viable signal set for an agent that calls 12 tools across 4 providers, with one tool hitting a flaky partner API? I don't think we have a canonical answer yet.
